Appointments and payments

Can I change my appointment?{{icon:chevron-down:18}}

Yes. You can reschedule a normal appointment twice within a year of your first appointment date.

A Tatkaal appointment can be rescheduled once. A Police Clearance Certificate appointment cannot be rescheduled.

Do I need to print my appointment receipt?{{icon:chevron-down:18}}

No. The SMS with your appointment details is accepted as proof of your appointment.

How long is my payment valid?{{icon:chevron-down:18}}

One year from the date you paid. If you have booked an appointment, it is one year from your first appointment date.

A payment cannot be moved from one application to another.

I paid but could not book an appointment{{icon:chevron-down:18}}

Sign in and use Schedule Appointment. You can check your payment with Track Payment Status.

Money left my account but the payment failed{{icon:chevron-down:18}}

SBI refunds failed payments to your bank within 7 working days. If you have not received it after that, contact the SBI helpdesk with your application reference number, transaction ID, amount and date.

Do I need an SBI account to pay?{{icon:chevron-down:18}}

No. You can pay with a card or UPI from any bank, or with net banking from SBI and other banks.

How police verification works

Police verification checks that the details in your application are correct, so a passport does not reach the wrong person.

Your Passport Office decides which type you need:

Before your passport is issued
Most new and re-issue applications
After your passport is issued
Tatkaal applications, and government employees with a No Objection Certificate (Annexure G)
Not needed
Usually children whose parent has a valid passport and whose parents both consent (Annexure D), and government employees with an Identity Certificate (Annexure A)

You only need proof of your present address. But you must list every address you have lived at in the last year, so the police can check them.

If you are from Jammu and Kashmir and live elsewhere, police verification is done at both your present and permanent addresses.

If your police report is adverse

Contact your Passport Office to find out why. Once the issue is cleared up, you can ask for police verification to be done again.

If police verification is taking a long time

If you have not received your passport, visit your Passport Office.

ePassports

An ePassport is a paper passport with an electronic chip inside. The chip holds your personal details and biometric information, digitally signed so border officials anywhere can check it is genuine.

You can recognise an ePassport by a small gold symbol on the front cover.

You do not need to replace your current passport. It stays valid until it expires. When your Passport Office starts issuing ePassports, new passports from that office will be ePassports.

Accessibility statement

This prototype is designed to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at level AA and the Guidelines for Indian Government Websites (GIGW) 3.0. You should be able to:

  • make text larger with the A+ button at the top of every page, or zoom up to 400% without content overlapping
  • switch to a high contrast colour scheme
  • use the whole website with a keyboard, with a clearly visible focus
  • use the website with a screen reader such as NVDA, JAWS, VoiceOver or TalkBack
  • use the website without JavaScript

Known issues

  • Some links go to PDF documents on the official website that may not be accessible.
  • The Hindi version is only available on the official website.
