PassportsGet a passport urgently (Tatkaal)

Tatkaal is a faster service for new and re-issued passports. It costs ₹2,500 on top of the normal passport fee.

Total fee
from ₹5,000 for an adult’s 36-page passport
When your passport is sent
usually the 3rd working day after it is granted
Police verification
after your passport is issued
Rescheduling
once, within a year of your first appointment

How Tatkaal works

Tatkaal applications are processed faster and printed in a separate queue. Police verification happens after the passport is issued, not before.

When you book, you can choose a Tatkaal or a Normal appointment slot. If no Tatkaal slot suits you, you can take a Normal slot. You still pay the Tatkaal fee, and your application is still processed under Tatkaal.

Your passport office makes the final decision on whether to issue a passport under Tatkaal.

Documents you need

You do not need a verification certificate from a Gazetted Officer. Instead, bring originals of:

  • any 3 documents from the list below if you are 18 or over
  • any 2 documents from the list if you are under 18

Also bring the usual proof of address and date of birth, and Non-ECR proof if it applies to you.

Documents accepted for Tatkaal
  1. Aadhaar card or e-Aadhaar
  2. Electors Photo Identity Card (EPIC)
  3. Service photo identity card from a State or Central Government body, PSU, local body or public limited company
  4. Scheduled Caste, Scheduled Tribe or Other Backward Class certificate
  5. Arms licence
  6. Pension document, such as an ex-serviceman’s pension book or pension payment order
  7. Your last passport (re-issue only)
  8. PAN card
  9. Bank, Kisan or Post Office passbook
  10. Student photo identity card from a recognised educational institution
  11. Driving licence from the state where you are applying
  12. Birth certificate
  13. Ration card

Sign the Tatkaal undertaking

Download, fill in and sign the undertaking, then bring it to your appointment.

Warning: If any Tatkaal document is missing or not in order, your application will be put on hold. It will be closed if the documents are not provided within 90 days.

Who cannot apply under Tatkaal

Some applicants cannot use Tatkaal. Check the list before you pay:

  • Categories not eligible for Tatkaal – English (PDF){{ext}}
  • Categories not eligible for Tatkaal – Hindi (PDF){{ext}}

For example, you cannot use Tatkaal if you live in Nagaland, or if your passport is damaged so badly that the number, name or photo cannot be read.
