Apply for a passport or renew one

Use this service to apply for a new Indian passport, or to renew or re-issue one, for an adult or a child.

You can also use it to change the details in your passport or replace one that has been lost, stolen or damaged.

Your current passport stays valid until its expiry date. You do not need to replace it when ePassports become available at your Passport Office.

How long it takes

Most applications need police verification before the passport is issued. How long that takes depends on your local police station.

If you need a passport urgently, apply under the Tatkaal scheme. Once a Tatkaal application is granted, the passport is usually dispatched on the third working day, not counting the day you applied.

!

Warning: Do not book travel until you have a valid passport. A re-issued passport has a new number.

Before you start

You will need:

  • proof of your present address and date of birth
  • a debit or credit card, net banking or UPI to pay the fee online
  • a mobile number and email address
  • your old passport, if you are renewing or re-issuing one

You do not need to provide a photo. Your photo and fingerprints are taken at your appointment.

The fee you pay is valid for one year. It cannot be moved to a different application.

The main steps

{{icon:clock:18}} About 20 minutes online, then one visit to a Passport Seva Kendra.

  1. Register on Passport Seva

    Create an account and choose the Passport Office for your present address. Find your Passport Office.

  2. Fill in the application form

    Choose Fresh for your first passport or Re-issue if you have had one before. Your details must match your documents exactly.

    You will be asked for every address you have lived at in the last year, for police verification.

  3. Pay and book an appointment

    Pay online by card, net banking, UPI or SBI wallet, then choose an appointment at a Passport Seva Kendra (PSK) or Post Office Passport Seva Kendra (POPSK).

    You can reschedule twice within a year of your first appointment. Tatkaal appointments can be rescheduled once.

  4. Go to your appointment

    Bring your original documents and one set of self-attested photocopies. Your photo and fingerprints will be taken.

    You do not need to print your appointment receipt. The confirmation SMS is enough.

  5. Police verification

    The police check the details you gave. This usually happens before your passport is issued. How police verification works.

  6. Receive your passport

    Your passport is sent to you by Speed Post. Track your application to see where it has reached.

Renew or re-issue a passport

Apply for a re-issue if:

  • your passport has expired or will expire soon
  • the visa pages are full
  • your name, address or other details have changed
  • you want the Emigration Check Required (ECR) status removed
  • it has been lost, stolen or damaged (see below)

A re-issue costs the same as a new passport: ₹2,500 for 36 pages or ₹3,500 for 60 pages. Check the fee for your situation.

Take your old passport to your appointment. It will be cancelled and returned to you.

If you have changed your name after marriage or divorce

To add your spouse’s name or change your surname, bring your marriage certificate, or a joint photo declaration signed by both of you (Annexure J).

To remove a spouse’s name, bring the divorce decree. To change it, also bring the remarriage certificate or Annexure J.

Passports for children

A passport for a child under 18 is valid for 5 years, or until they turn 18 if that is sooner. It has 36 pages and costs ₹1,750.

Children aged 15 to 17 can choose a 10-year passport instead, at the adult fee.

  • Both parents are assumed to consent unless the form says otherwise.
  • Proof of address can be in a parent’s name, and parents can attest the documents.
  • Bring both parents’ passports, if they have them.
  • A child born on or after 1 October 2023 must use their birth certificate as proof of date of birth.

Police verification is usually not needed if either parent has a valid passport and both parents consent using Annexure D.

Children aged 8 or under get a 10% discount on the basic fee for a new passport.

Lost, stolen or damaged passports

Report a lost or stolen passport to the police straight away, and take a copy of the police report to your appointment.

Replacing a passport costs more than renewing one:

  • ₹5,000 for a 36-page passport
  • ₹6,000 for a 60-page passport
  • ₹4,250 for a child’s 36-page passport

If your passport is damaged but the passport number, name and photo can still be read, you can apply for a re-issue under Tatkaal. If it is damaged beyond recognition, you cannot use Tatkaal.

Other ways to apply

On your phone

Use the mPassport Seva app{{ext}} to apply, pay, book and track. It is available for Android and iPhone.

From outside India

Apply through the Indian embassy, high commission or consulate{{ext}} in the country where you live.

With help

If you cannot apply online, call the National Call Centre on 1800-258-1800. Only use the official website: agents who charge to fill in the form are not needed.
