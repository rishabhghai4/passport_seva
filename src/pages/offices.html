Find a Passport Office

There are 37 Regional Passport Offices in India, and more than 500 places where you can have your appointment.

Your Passport Office depends on your present address. It is chosen for you when you register on Passport Seva.

Types of office

  • Regional Passport Office (RPO)

    Checks and grants applications for its area. Visit if you are asked to, for example about a pending or held application.

  • Passport Seva Kendra (PSK)

    Where most people go for their appointment. Your documents are checked and your photo and fingerprints are taken.

  • Post Office Passport Seva Kendra (POPSK)

    A Passport Seva Kendra at a head post office, so you can have your appointment closer to home.
