Passport fees

Passport fees changed on 1 July 2026. You pay online when you book your appointment.

Important

An adult’s 36-page passport now costs ₹2,500, up from ₹1,500.

A 60-page passport now costs ₹3,500, and Tatkaal adds ₹2,500 to any passport fee.

Discount for young children and senior citizens

For a new passport (not a re-issue), you get 10% off the basic passport fee if the applicant is 8 or under, or over 60.

There is no fee for diplomatic or official passports.

How to pay

You must pay online before you can book an appointment. You can pay by:

  • debit or credit card (Visa, Mastercard or RuPay)
  • net banking (SBI and other banks)
  • UPI
  • SBI wallet

Your payment is valid for one year from the date you paid, or from your first appointment if you have booked one. It cannot be moved to another application.

If money left your account but the payment shows as failed or pending

If the status is Pending, check again on the next working day using Track Payment Status on your Applicant Home page.

If the status is Failed, SBI will refund your bank within 7 working days. If it does not, email customercare.00691@sbi.co.in or call 011-41561114 (10am to 5pm) with your application reference number, transaction ID, amount and date.

If you paid twice, the Regional Passport Office will refund the extra payment.
