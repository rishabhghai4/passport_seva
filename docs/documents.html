Documents you need for a passport

Bring the original of each document, and one set of self-attested photocopies, to your appointment.

Most adults need one proof of present address and one proof of date of birth. Some people need extra documents.

Your Passport Office may ask for other documents. Check its page on the list of Passport Offices before your appointment.

Proof of present address – bring one
  • Aadhaar card
  • Electors Photo Identity Card (EPIC)
  • water, electricity, telephone (landline or post-paid mobile) or gas connection bill
  • income tax assessment order
  • registered rent agreement
  • passbook of a running bank account with a scheduled public sector, private sector or regional rural bank, with your photo
  • certificate from your employer on letterhead, if it is a reputed company
  • your spouse’s passport, if it names you as spouse and shows your present address
  • a parent’s passport, for children
Proof of date of birth – bring one

If you were born on or after 1 October 2023, you must use your birth certificate.

If you were born before 1 October 2023, you can use:

  • a birth certificate from the Registrar of Births and Deaths or the Municipal Corporation
  • a transfer, school leaving or matriculation certificate showing your date of birth
  • PAN card
  • driving licence
  • Electors Photo Identity Card (EPIC)
  • a Life Insurance Corporation or public company policy bond
  • an extract of your service record or pension payment order, if you work or worked for the government

A PAN card, driving licence, EPIC or service record is only accepted if it shows your exact date of birth.

Non-ECR proof – if it applies to you

Emigration Check Required (ECR) status means you need clearance to work in some countries. You can get Non-ECR status if, for example, you have passed Class 10 or higher.

Bring proof of the category you belong to, such as your Class 10 certificate. See all Non-ECR categories and documents (opens the official Passport Seva website).

Extra documents for children
  • a parent’s proof of address can be used
  • parents’ passports, originals and self-attested copies, if they have them
  • Annexure D, signed by both parents, to avoid police verification when a parent has a valid passport

Parents can attest their child’s documents.

Extra documents for Tatkaal

Adults need any 3, and under 18s any 2, documents from the Tatkaal list, plus a signed Tatkaal undertaking. See the Tatkaal document list.

If your name has changed after marriage or divorce
  • to add a spouse’s name: marriage certificate or Annexure J (joint photo declaration)
  • to remove a spouse’s name: divorce decree
  • to change a spouse’s name: divorce decree or death certificate, plus remarriage certificate or Annexure J

If you are changing your whole name, follow the standard name change process.

If you work for the government, a PSU or a statutory body

Bring one of these from your employer. It decides when police verification happens:

  • Identity Certificate (Annexure A): no police verification
  • No Objection Certificate (Annexure G): police verification after your passport is issued
  • Prior Intimation Letter (Annexure H): police verification before your passport is issued

Dependent family members can also use an Identity Certificate (Annexure A).
