    What your status means

    1. Application submitted

      Your documents, photo and fingerprints were accepted at your appointment.

    2. Police verification in progress

      Your local police station is checking your details. They may visit you or ask you to visit them.

    3. Police verification report received

      The report has reached the Passport Office and is being reviewed.

    4. Passport granted

      Your application has been approved and sent for printing.

    5. Passport printed

      Your passport has been printed and is being prepared for posting.

    6. Passport dispatched

      Your passport has been sent by Speed Post. The status shows a tracking number you can use on the India Post website.

    If your application is on hold

    The Passport Office needs something from you, usually a document. Check your SMS and email, and bring what is asked for to the Passport Seva Kendra as soon as you can.

    If your police report is adverse

    Contact your Passport Office to find out why. If it can be resolved, you can ask for police verification to be done again.

    If you have waited a long time

    Call the National Call Centre on 1800-258-1800, or register a grievance (opens the official Passport Seva website).
