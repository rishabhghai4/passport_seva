Only apply on the official website

Fake websites such as indiapassport.org and passport-seva.in collect personal data and charge extra fees. The only official websites are passportindia.gov.in and the mPassport Seva app. Government fees are paid online only, and nobody needs to fill in your form for you.

Notices and updates

  • NewFees

    Passport fees revised from 1 July 2026

    A 36-page passport now costs ₹2,500 and a 60-page passport ₹3,500. Tatkaal adds ₹2,500 to any passport fee.

    Check you are eligible before applying under Tatkaal
